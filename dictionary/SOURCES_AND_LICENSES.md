# Diccionario Vivo — Source and licensing notes

This teaching reference combines two independent FreeDict sources with original classroom-facing interface text. Source records remain identified by direction and are **not** reclassified as original dictionary definitions.

- **Spanish–English FreeDict, version 0.3.1:** GPL-2.0-or-later. Upstream: https://freedict.org/ and https://download.freedict.org/dictionaries/spa-eng/0.3.1/
- **English–Spanish FreeDict / WikDict, version 2025.11.23:** CC BY-SA 3.0 Unported. Upstream: https://freedict.org/ and https://download.freedict.org/dictionaries/eng-spa/2025.11.23/
- Source archives are fetched at build time from their pinned release URLs and SHA-256 checked. Students receive static files from Ak's Spanish Class, not cross-origin dictionary requests.
- The existing classroom examples and learning labels are displayed from the site's own Language Lab material and are not attributed to FreeDict. The short synonym suggestions on the page are teaching examples, not a complete licensed thesaurus.

**Interpretation limitations:** English → Spanish records include word senses and possible translations. These may be useful reverse Spanish lookups but are *not* individually verified Spanish meanings, synonyms or grammatical labels. The page limits lengthy previews and search results; the original archives are the authority for complete records. Dictionary lookup does not perform sentence translation.

**Pronunciation limitations:** The separate Alphabet & IPA page describes broad Spanish phonemes and regional differences. It does not provide individually verified IPA for every indexed Spanish string. English IPA in the English-source dictionary refers to the English headword, not its Spanish translation.

The upstream works have separate licenses; redistribution and derivative-work obligations must be respected. For a commercial or more widely distributed derivative, get a license review before combining data into a single rewritten edition.
