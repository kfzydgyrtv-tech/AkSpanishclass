Spanish subject pronouns

Unit 1 reference. Use the subject pronoun to choose the matching verb form.

PersonSingularPlural
First yo (I) nosotras (all female)
nosotros (male or mixed group)
Second tú (informal you)
vos (informal you in some regions)
usted (formal you)		 vosotras (informal, all female)
vosotros (informal, male or mixed group)
ustedes (plural you)
Third ella (she)
él (he)		 ellas (all female)
ellos (male or mixed group)

Vosotras and vosotros are used mainly in Spain. In Latin America, ustedes is the usual plural form of you in formal and informal speech.
