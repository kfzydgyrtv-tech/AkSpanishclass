Accent marks and stress

Vowels, -n, -s

Words ending in a vowel, n, or s usually stress the penultimate syllable if no written accent tells you otherwise.

ca-sa · ha-blan
Other endings

Words ending in other consonants usually stress the final syllable if unmarked.

pa-pel · re-loj
Written accents

A written accent overrides the default stress rule and may distinguish meanings.

mú-si-ca · es-tá
